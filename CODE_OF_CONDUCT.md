# Code of Conduct

SparkDown follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space,
and when you represent the project elsewhere.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep
criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and
publishing someone's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, contact the
maintainer privately through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/paulovitorjp).
Do not use public issues for conduct reports. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and decides on a response, from a
private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project, following
the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
